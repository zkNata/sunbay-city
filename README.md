# Sunbay City

An original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, playable offline in a desktop browser. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty.

You are Rook — new in Sunbay City, broke, and already on Marta's list. Explore the city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Sunbay PD as your wanted level rises, and work through 4 story missions: Welcome to Sunbay, Hot Wheels, Package Run, and The Marina Job.

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

Controls: WASD / arrows — move and drive · E — enter car / talk · F — punch · Mouse — aim and shoot · Shift — run · P — pause · M — mute. Progress saves automatically.

## What's inside

- A sun-soaked coastal city: roads, traffic, pedestrians, a park, a beach and a marina
- Cars to steal and drive, with a wanted level (1–5 stars) and police who chase and bust you
- 4 story missions with objectives, timers and payouts, plus combat and progression
- HUD with health, cash, wanted stars and clock, and a live minimap
- Synthesised WebAudio music and sound effects — no network requests, no external assets

Everything in this game — the city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art, sound and music — is original. Nothing is taken from any commercial game.
